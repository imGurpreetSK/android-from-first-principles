# Android from First Principles

Android from First Principles is a free online series. It explains the machinery under Android app development, one chapter at a time. For example, it explains why the main thread is a loop and what happens between a launcher tap and the first frame. The default reader is a mid-level Android developer with 1–4 years of experience. Each non-obvious claim has a citation.

## AI disclosure

AI drafts most chapters from a source list that the maintainer approves. Then a human edits and checks each chapter. Each non-obvious claim links to its source. If you find an error, challenge the claim.

## Challenge a claim

Anyone can challenge a claim. Open a "Challenge a claim" issue. In the issue, give the chapter, the claim and a counter-source. You can also send a fix PR. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) gives the details.
