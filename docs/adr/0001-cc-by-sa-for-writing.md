# Writing is CC BY-SA 4.0, not CC BY-NC-SA

Outside contributors can write whole chapters, and there is no contributor agreement, so each contributor keeps copyright on their chapter and the maintainer only gets the license the repo grants. Under CC BY-NC-SA that would have made every contributed chapter off-limits for any future book, course or sponsored edition without written permission from each contributor. We chose CC BY-SA 4.0 for all writing so commercial use is open to everyone, the maintainer included. Code in `samples/` stays MIT.

## Considered options

- **CC BY-NC-SA, no agreement:** stops others selling the content, but contributed chapters can never be used commercially by the maintainer either.
- **CC BY-NC-SA plus a relicense grant in `CONTRIBUTING.md`:** keeps non-commercial for others, but adds a legal step to every contribution.
- **CC BY-SA (chosen):** no agreement needed, no commercial dead end.

## Consequences

- Anyone may republish or sell the writing, as long as they credit it and share it under the same license.
- Hard to reverse: once contributed chapters are merged under BY-SA, moving to a non-commercial license would need every contributor's consent.
