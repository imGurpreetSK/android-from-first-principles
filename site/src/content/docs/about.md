## AI disclosure

AI drafts most chapters from a source list that the maintainer approves. Two AI subagents, a fact checker and a staff reviewer, review each draft. Then a human edits and checks each chapter. Each non-obvious claim links to its source. If you find an error, challenge the claim.

## Challenge a claim

Anyone can challenge a claim. Open a "Challenge a claim" issue. In the issue, give the chapter, the claim and a counter-source. You can also send a fix PR. [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/imGurpreetSK/android-from-first-principles/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) gives the details.
